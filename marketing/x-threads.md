**1/**
My Google Photos was full of screenshots, bursts and 2-second pocket videos.

So I built Vault Tidy: a free Chrome extension that finds them with an AI model running on your own computer.

It only suggests. It never deletes. 🧵

**2/**
Open Google Photos, click the icon, and it groups what it finds:

📱 screenshots & shopping pages
🧾 receipts & documents
😂 memes
🌫️ blurry, dark or accidental shots
📸 near-duplicate bursts (keeps the sharpest)
🎬 tiny clips, screen recordings, long videos

**3/**
Privacy, because it's your photos:
• the CLIP model ships inside the extension
• no servers, no account, no analytics
• nothing is uploaded anywhere
• code is open source (MIT)

**4/**
You review and decide. Tick what you don't need → cleanup list (links or CSV), or one private "to remove" album (beta) so you can trash them in Google Photos yourself.

Everything has Undo.

**5/**
Honest bits: it's beta, Chrome on a computer only, ~230 MB (the model is bundled), and it reads the Google Photos site, so it may break when Google changes it.

Not affiliated with Google.
