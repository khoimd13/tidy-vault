How it works

Vault Tidy opens in Chrome's side panel next to Google Photos and reads what that tab shows.

  1. Open Google Photos

    Your library, a search such as "screenshots", or an album. Then click the Vault Tidy icon.

  2. Find suggestions

    It scrolls through the view while an AI model on your computer looks at each thumbnail. Later scans only read new photos.

  3. Review

    "Likely clutter" starts selected; untick what you want to keep. Bursts keep their sharpest shot. Videos play right in the panel.

  4. Clean up yourself

    Copy links, export a CSV, or put them in one new private album (beta) and move it to trash in Google Photos.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no paid tier.

Will it delete my photos?

No. Vault Tidy only suggests. You choose what goes on your cleanup list, and you remove photos yourself in Google Photos. If you use the optional "to remove" album (beta), Vault Tidy adds your chosen photos to one new private album; deleting that album later keeps all its photos. Google Photos keeps trashed items for 60 days.

Is Vault Tidy made by Google?

No. It's an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google. Google Photos is a trademark of Google LLC.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no Vault Tidy account. It uses the Google Photos tab you're already signed in to.

How accurate is the AI?

It's good at screenshots (in testing, all real screenshots were rated High and no real photo was). Other categories are less certain, which is why "Not sure" photos are never preselected and you always review first. It also learns from what you keep.

Why is the download so big?

About 230 MB, because the AI is built in, so nothing has to be downloaded or sent anywhere while you use it.

What are the limits?

It's beta. It works in Chrome on a computer, not on phones. It reads the Google Photos website, so when Google changes its site, parts may stop working until an update; it fails safely without changing anything. Photo dates are read only when Google Photos is set to English or Vietnamese.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Tiếng Việt, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Deutsch, Français, Español and Português.

Something went wrong. Where do I report it?

Use the Support tab on the Chrome Web Store page. Telling us what kind of photo was misjudged helps the most.
