## Text

> I made a free Chrome extension that goes through your Google Photos and suggests what you
> probably don't need: screenshots, near-duplicate bursts, blurry or dark shots, receipts,
> memes and 2-second pocket videos. It never deletes anything. You review the suggestions and
> remove photos yourself.
>
> The AI runs on your own computer. It is built into the extension, so your photos are not
> uploaded anywhere. There's no server, no account and no analytics.
>
> A few things I learned while building it:
>
> - A photo is only flagged when it looks more like clutter than like a memory, so selfies
>   and landscapes are left alone.
> - For bursts it keeps the sharpest photo and suggests the rest.
> - Long videos are only listed as "low" because they are often precious.
> - Videos can be played right in the side panel, so you can check before deciding.
>
> Limits: it's beta. It reads the Google Photos website, so it can break when Google changes
> the site. The download is about 230 MB because the AI is built in. It's an independent
> project, not affiliated with Google.
>
> I'd love feedback on what it gets wrong in your library.

## If people ask

- **Does it delete photos?** No. It only suggests. You delete photos yourself in Google Photos.
- **How accurate is it?** In my tests, all real screenshots were rated High and no real photo
  was. Other groups are less certain, so "Not sure" photos are never selected for you.
- **Why so big?** The AI is built in, so nothing has to be downloaded or sent while you use it.
