## Description (≈260 characters)

> Vault Tidy is a free Chrome extension that finds screenshots, near-duplicate bursts, blurry
> shots, receipts, memes and stray videos in Google Photos. An AI model runs on your computer:
> nothing is uploaded and nothing is deleted. You review and decide. No account.

## First maker comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I'm [your first name], and I made Vault Tidy because my Google Photos was full of
> screenshots, 12-shot bursts and pocket videos, and I didn't want to hand my whole photo
> library to another cloud service to sort it.
>
> **What it does**
> Open Google Photos, click the Vault Tidy icon, and it scrolls through whatever you're
> viewing (your library, a search, an album). An AI running _on your computer_ sorts
> what it finds into groups: screenshots, shopping pages, memes, receipts and documents,
> whiteboards, blurry/dark/empty shots, accidental shots, near-duplicate bursts (it keeps the
> sharpest), and short, long or screen-recorded videos. It shows how much space you could
> free, using real file sizes where Google provides them.
>
> **What it doesn't do**
> It never deletes anything. You tick what you don't need and get a cleanup list (links or
> CSV). If you want, it can put those photos into one new private album (beta) so you can
> remove them in Google Photos in a few clicks. Everything has Undo.
>
> **Privacy**
> No servers, no account, no analytics. The AI is built into the extension (that's why
> it's ~230 MB), so your photos never leave your computer.
>
> **Honest limits**
> It's beta. It reads the Google Photos web page, so when Google changes its site, parts can
> break until I ship a fix (it fails safely). Dates are read only when Google Photos is in
> English or Vietnamese for now. It's an independent project, not affiliated with Google.
>
> The panel speaks 12 languages, keeps each Google account's results separate, and plays
> videos right in the panel. Next up: protecting photos with faces and learning more from
> your choices.
>
> I'd love to hear what it gets wrong in your library. That's the most useful feedback.
