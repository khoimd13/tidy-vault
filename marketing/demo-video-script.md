Format: 1920×1080 screen recording, 30 fps, captions burned in (most people watch muted).
Export a 1080×1080 crop for X/Threads/Facebook. Music: royalty-free, quiet.

Before recording:

- Use a **test Google account** filled with sample photos (screenshots, a burst of 6–8 near
  copies, a few blurry/dark shots, a receipt, a meme, a 2 s clip). No real personal photos.
- Blur or crop the email shown at the top of the panel.
- Keep the Google Photos logo out of frame (crop the top-left) and don't show Google's logo
  anywhere. Chrome at 100% zoom, a clean profile, no other extensions visible.
